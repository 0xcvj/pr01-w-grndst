Terms

These terms outline how GRND Studio typically scopes, delivers, and supports custom software development, AI-powered applications, automation systems, and related project work.

Updated: March 5, 2026

Project scope and proposals

Every engagement starts with a defined problem, a proposed scope, and a delivery approach.

Work is governed by the written proposal, scope, timeline, or agreement accepted for the specific engagement. That documentation defines what GRND Studio is building, whether the work covers strategy, custom software, AI implementation, automation, integrations, support, or phased delivery, and what is outside the agreed scope.

Client responsibilities

Delivery depends on timely access, feedback, and decision-making from the client side.

Clients are responsible for providing accurate project information, timely approvals, access to required systems, and any third-party credentials or materials needed for delivery. Delays in feedback, access, or approvals can affect timelines and milestone planning.

Payments, ownership, and support

Commercial terms are set per project, not assumed from the public website alone.

Payment schedules, intellectual property transfer, licensing, maintenance, and support commitments are defined in the applicable project agreement. Unless stated otherwise in writing, GRND Studio retains ownership of pre-existing methods, reusable frameworks, internal tooling, and other background intellectual property used to deliver the work.

Liability and governing documents

This page is a high-level summary and does not replace project-specific signed terms.

Each engagement may include additional terms covering confidentiality, data handling, warranties, acceptance, and allocation of risk. Where a signed agreement exists, that agreement governs the relationship. If you have a question about current terms for a proposed project, contact us before work begins.
