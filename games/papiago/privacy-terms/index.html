Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

PapiaGo is a language-learning application focused on Papiamento — the creole language spoken on Aruba, (Papiamentu) on Bonaire, and Curaçao. PapiaGo is developed and operated by GRND Studio.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at support@grnd.studio.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a PapiaGo account, we may collect your display name, email address, and profile picture. You can use the app without creating an account, but some features (progress sync, leaderboards) require one.

Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the app — such as lessons completed, quiz scores, streaks, and feature interactions. This data is not sold to third parties.

Device Information

We may collect device type, operating system version, app version, and general location (country-level) for analytics and crash reporting.

Audio & Sound Playback

PapiaGo may include audio playback features for pronunciation and listening exercises. Audio is played locally on your device. We do not record, store, or transmit any audio from your device's microphone unless a future feature explicitly requires it, in which case we will update this policy and request your permission first.

  • To personalise lesson content and difficulty based on your level
  • To power leaderboards, streaks, and achievement features
  • To process subscription payments via the App Store or Google Play
  • To send important service-related notifications (e.g. account security)
  • To analyse aggregated, anonymised usage trends

PapiaGo is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, contact us at support@grnd.studio and we will promptly delete it.
