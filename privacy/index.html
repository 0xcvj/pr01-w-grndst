Privacy Policy

You reach out, we use the details you share to reply, scope the work, and decide if we are the right fit. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

Effective

March 5, 2026

Plain English. No extra fluff. Just how GRND Studio handles the information you send us.

Overview

What we collect

We only collect what helps us understand your enquiry and respond properly.

If you fill out the form, email us, or contact us about a project, we may collect your name, email address, company name, and the project details you choose to share. That can include your current tools, bottlenecks, goals, timelines, and other context needed to understand the work.

Use

How data is used

We use your information to reply, scope the work, and keep the conversation moving.

We use what you send us to respond to your enquiry, assess fit, ask better follow-up questions, prepare proposals, and manage project communication. We do not sell contact data or use project enquiries for unrelated bulk marketing.

Handling

Tools, retention, and protection

Some messages pass through normal business tools. Nothing more than that.

Messages submitted through the site may pass through trusted form, email, hosting, and analytics providers that support the website and our enquiry workflow. We keep information for as long as needed to respond, assess opportunities, manage active discussions, or meet legal and administrative obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transfer or storage system is completely risk-free.

Control

Your choices and contact

If you want something corrected or removed, ask us directly.

You can contact GRND Studio to request correction or deletion of the information you have shared with us, subject to any legal or administrative reason we may need to keep part of the record. For privacy questions, email info@grnd.studio.
