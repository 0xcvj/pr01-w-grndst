Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GRND Studio handles information shared through our website when you contact us about custom software development, AI-powered applications, automation systems, or related advisory work.

Effective date: March 5, 2026

What we collect

We keep the information collection narrow and tied to business communication.

When you submit a form, email us, or contact us about a potential project, we may collect your name, email address, company name, and the project or operational details you choose to share. That can include information about your current tools, business processes, goals, and timelines.

How data is used

We use submitted information to evaluate fit and respond to real project requests.

We use your information to reply to your enquiry, assess whether we are a good fit for your software or AI project, prepare follow-up questions, and manage service-related communication. We do not use contact submissions for unrelated resale or mass-list marketing.

Tools, retention, and protection

Project enquiries may pass through standard business tools, but only for the purpose of handling the request.

Messages submitted through the site may be processed through trusted form, email, hosting, and analytics providers that support the operation of the website and our enquiry workflow. We retain information only for as long as needed to respond, assess opportunities, manage ongoing discussions, or satisfy legal and administrative obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect submitted data, but no internet transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and contact

If you want us to update or remove information you submitted, contact us directly.

You can contact GRND Studio to request correction or deletion of the information you have shared with us, subject to any legal or administrative reason we may need to retain part of the record. For privacy-related questions, email info@grnd.studio.
