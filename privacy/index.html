Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

GRND Studio is a sole proprietorship based in the Netherlands. This policy covers the website grnd.studio and the tools and integrations GRND Studio operates, including its Google Drive backup integration. Questions: info@grnd.studio.

This website

grnd.studio is a static website. We do not use tracking cookies or sell personal data. If you contact us or fill in a form, we only use what you send us to reply to you, and we keep it no longer than needed for that purpose.

Google user data

GRND Studio uses an internal OAuth application to back up its own servers to Google Drive. It is used only by GRND Studio and is not offered to the public.

  • What we access: only files and folders that the application itself creates in Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other files in the account.
  • How we use it: to upload, list, download and delete encrypted backup files.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored only on GRND Studio's own machines. Backup data is encrypted before it leaves those machines.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

GRND Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for any data to be deleted, email info@grnd.studio.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
